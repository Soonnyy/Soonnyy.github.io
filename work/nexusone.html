简介Summary

Muru 银行要把零售存款和储蓄账户从两套老核心系统迁到新平台，预算 7000 万澳元、工期 25 个月，必须和另一个项目在同一个周末一起切换上线。这门课一整个学期都在跟这个项目：我主导搭建了 MS Project 执行基线（A1），独立完成挣值分析和一页式状态仪表盘（A2），在期末展示中讲项目概况和范围绩效（A4），并负责收尾报告的执行摘要、项目概述、结论和全文整合（A5）。

Muru Bank is moving retail transaction and savings accounts from two legacy cores to a new platform: AUD 70m, 25 months, and a single shared cutover weekend with another project. The unit followed this project all semester. I led the Microsoft Project baseline (A1), did the earned-value analysis and one-page status dashboard on my own (A2), presented the overview and scope performance (A4), and wrote the executive summary, overview and conclusion of the close-out report and integrated the full document (A5).

  1. 用到的技能：MS Project（WBS、逻辑关系、关键路径、资源和成本加载）、挣值管理（SPI、CPI、EAC）、状态报告、收尾与项目后评价
  2. 展现的能力：从数据里找出真正的问题；用一页纸向上汇报并提出需要决策的事项；把一个项目从计划跟到收尾
  1. Skills: Microsoft Project (WBS, logic links, critical path, resource and cost loading), earned value (SPI, CPI, EAC), status reporting, close-out and post-implementation review
  2. Shows: finding the real issue in the numbers, reporting upward on one page with decisions requested, following a project from plan to close

1. 背景1. Context

NexusOne 是 Muru 银行的 12 个相互关联的项目组成的项目群，我们组负责其中的 P3「核心存款与账户迁移」。它的难点不在技术本身，而在约束：余额、条款、利息费用和对账单历史一样都不能错；P3 和 P11 在 2029 年 1 月 28 日共用一次全行切换，要五个项目一起决定能不能上线；P3 不收尾，P12 就没法关停老系统。

NexusOne is Muru Bank's programme of 12 linked projects; our group had P3, Core Deposits and Accounts Migration. The challenge was the constraints rather than the technology: balances, terms, interest, fees and statement history must all be exact; P3 shares one enterprise cutover with P11 on 28 January 2029, with a five-project go/no-go; and P12 cannot retire the legacy systems until P3 closes.

2. 我做了什么2. What I did

A1 执行基线（小组作业，MS Project 基线主要由我搭建）A1 Execution baseline (group work; I built most of the Microsoft Project baseline)

  1. 159 项任务、4 层 WBS、7 个项目群强制里程碑，约 4.33 万工时，基线成本 6,691 万澳元（含 500 万不可预见费），预算余量 309 万。
  2. 发现里程碑被硬日期约束锁死，关键路径是假的。改成由逻辑关系驱动后，3 个关键里程碑的浮时为零，真实的关键路径才显示出来。
  3. 修正了一个负成本错误：人工成本从 157 万补到 641 万。
  4. 补上 P3 和三个兄弟项目之间遗漏的对账接口任务。
  1. 159 tasks, a 4-level WBS, 7 mandated programme milestones, about 43,300 hours and a AUD 66.91m baseline including AUD 5m contingency, leaving AUD 3.09m headroom.
  2. Found milestones pinned by hard date constraints, which made the critical path false. Switching to logic-driven links left three key milestones at zero float and exposed the real critical path.
  3. Fixed a negative-cost error, raising labour cost from AUD 1.57m to 6.41m.
  4. Added missing reconciliation interface tasks between P3 and three sibling projects.

A5 收尾与后评价报告（小组作业）A5 Close-out and post-implementation review (group work)

我写了执行摘要、第 1 章项目概述（背景、目标与评价框架、基线、关键相关方）和第 8 章结论，并负责全文整合。报告结论：P3 在 2029 年 1 月 28 日一次切换迁完全部账户，最终成本 6,841 万，比预算低 2.28%；两项获批变更（1,240 万的对账差额、停机窗口从 14 小时缩到 8 小时）在上线前就被控制关口发现并吸收，代价是 M4 晚了 6 个工作日、多花 150 万。

I wrote the executive summary, Chapter 1 (background, objectives and evaluation framework, baseline, key stakeholders) and the Chapter 8 conclusion, and integrated the document. Its finding: P3 migrated every in-scope account in one cutover on 28 January 2029 at AUD 68.41m, 2.28% under budget. Two approved changes (a AUD 12.4m reconciliation imbalance and an outage window cut from 14 to 8 hours) were caught by control gates before go-live, at the cost of a six-day M4 delay and AUD 1.5m.

3. 关键判断3. Key judgements

  1. 成本超支的 616 万里，真正超支的只有 205 万。CPI 0.871 看起来很糟，但我把成本偏差拆开：基线里 67% 是供应商许可、实施伙伴和工具这类合同承诺，钱按合同付出去了，可系统集成测试卡住，挣值还没赚回来。其中 411 万是「已付但未挣得」，测试完成后会转回挣值。这样管理层看到的是一个可以解决的进度问题，而不是失控的成本问题。
  2. 把汇报落到决策上。仪表盘最后列出请 PCSC 拍板的三件事：给实施伙伴追加合同额度；批准动用基线内的 500 万储备；确认 EAC 7,680 万，或者接受 TCPI 1.139 的追赶要求。
  3. 评价要有尺子。收尾报告开头先把 A1 定下的 5 条成功标准和项目群关口一一对应，再逐条对照结果，而不是笼统地说「项目成功」。
  1. Only AUD 2.05m of the 6.16m variance is a true overrun. CPI 0.871 looks bad, but 67% of the baseline is contracted vendor licence, partner and tooling cost, paid on schedule while integration testing stalled. AUD 4.11m is paid but not yet earned and converts back once testing completes. Executives see a fixable schedule problem, not runaway cost.
  2. Turn reporting into decisions. The dashboard ends with three PCSC decisions: a partner contract uplift, release of the AUD 5m reserve already in the baseline, and confirming EAC 76.8m or accepting a TCPI of 1.139.
  3. Evaluate against a yardstick. The close-out report first maps the five A1 success criteria to programme gates, then tests results against each, rather than declaring success in general terms.

5. 老师反馈与不足5. Feedback and limits

  1. A1 得分 71/100。老师肯定了 WBS 详细、基线完整保存；扣分在资源模型：项目经理分配的工时为 0，资源过载是靠调高资源上限消掉的，而不是真正调整排程；跨项目依赖也不全。现在回头看，资源过载应该用资源平衡或调整任务逻辑来解决，调高上限只是把问题藏起来了。
  2. A2、A4、A5 成绩还没公布。
  3. 模拟项目。A5 的收尾结果是小组基于基线、状态报告和课程情景商定的模拟结局，不是真实项目数据。
  1. A1 scored 71/100. The marker praised the detailed WBS and saved baseline; marks were lost on the resource model: zero hours for the PM, and over-allocation removed by raising resource capacity rather than re-scheduling, plus incomplete cross-project dependencies. In hindsight, over-allocation should be resolved by levelling or task logic; raising capacity only hides it.
  2. A2, A4 and A5 marks are pending.
  3. A simulation. A5 outcomes are a close-out scenario agreed by the group from the baseline, status reports and course scenarios, not real project data.

6. 和实际工作的关系6. Why it matters in practice

一个学期从计划、监控到收尾跟完同一个项目，最大的收获是：数字本身不说话，要拆开看。CPI 低不一定是花多了，可能是付款节奏和挣值节奏不一致。我在实习中做过结算审计，这种「先拆清楚钱花在哪、再下结论」的习惯在两边都用得上。

Following one project from plan through control to close-out taught me that numbers do not speak for themselves; they need to be broken down. A low CPI may reflect payment timing rather than overspend. I did final-account audit in my internship, and the habit of breaking down where money went before concluding applies in both places.
