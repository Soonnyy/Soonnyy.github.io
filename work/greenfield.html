Project Greenfield：880 万澳元社区体育馆的成本、质量与资源计划Project Greenfield: Cost, Quality and Resource Plans for a AUD 8.8m Sports Pavilion

简介Summary

市政府要拆掉 Greenfield 运动场里老旧的场馆用房，新建一座约 1,200 m² 的单层社区体育馆，总资金 880 万澳元，工期约一年。小组为它写完整的项目管理计划，我负责成本、质量和资源三章：成本估算和不可预见费、分时段预算和现金流、挣值控制规则；质量标准、质检流程和质量成本；组织结构、人员配置、资源直方图和 RACI。合稿时我还发现排程里施工期的内部人员是零，补上了人员计划。

The council is replacing an ageing pavilion with a single-storey community sports pavilion of about 1,200 m², with AUD 8.8m funding and about a year to build. Our group wrote the full project management plan; I wrote cost, quality and resources: estimates and contingency, time-phased budget and cash flow, earned-value rules; quality standards, assurance, control and cost of quality; organisation, staffing, resource histogram and RACI. At merge I also found the schedule showed zero in-house staff during construction and added a staffing plan.

  1. 用到的技能：自下而上与参数估算、风险量化定不可预见费（EMV）、分时段预算与 S 曲线、挣值阈值、质量成本（COQ）、资源直方图、RACI、MS Project 资源分配
  2. 展现的能力：每个数字都说得出依据；在合稿时发现跨章节的矛盾并补上
  1. Skills: bottom-up and parametric estimating, risk-based contingency (EMV), time-phased budget and S-curve, earned-value thresholds, cost of quality, resource histogram, RACI, MS Project resource assignment
  2. Shows: a justified basis for every number; catching cross-chapter inconsistencies at merge and fixing them

1. 背景1. Context

Greenfield 体育馆要服务体育俱乐部、学校和社区团体，采用混合交付模式：施工外包给承包商，市政府自己负责管理和监督。资金上限 880 万澳元固定不变，其中 10 万是市政府定的管理储备。所以计划要回答三个问题：钱怎么分、怎么控；质量怎么保证；市政府自己要派多少人、什么时候派。

The pavilion serves sporting clubs, schools and community groups, under a hybrid model: construction is contracted out while the council manages and supervises. Funding is capped at AUD 8.8m, including a council-set AUD 100,000 management reserve. The plan had to answer how money is split and controlled, how quality is assured, and how many in-house staff are needed and when.

2. 我的思路2. How I approached it

成本（第 4 章）Cost (Chapter 4)

  1. 按工作分解结构分 5 个控制账户，每个账户用合适的估算方法：设计和拆除用自下而上；主体建筑用参数法（420 万 ÷ 1,200 m² = 每平方米 3,500 澳元），再请工料测量师对照公开造价指南核实。
  2. 不可预见费按风险量化来定，不套经验比例。风险登记册应对前的期望损失是 41.75 万，不可预见费取 50 万，覆盖 120%，占直接成本 6.6%，低于行业常用的 10%，但每一块钱都有来源。
  3. 预算按月分布，画出 S 曲线，对照市政府的拨款节奏检查每个月的现金是否够用。
  4. 控制规则写成具体数字：CPI 或 SPI 低于 0.95、任一控制账户偏差超过 2.5 万就要调查；剩余风险期望值超过不可预见费的一半（25 万），项目经理必须上报指导委员会。
  1. Five control accounts on the WBS, each with a fitting method: bottom-up for design and demolition; parametric for the building (AUD 4.2m ÷ 1,200 m² = AUD 3,500/m²), to be checked by the QS against published cost guides.
  2. Contingency from quantified risk, not a rule of thumb. Pre-response EMV was AUD 417,500; contingency of AUD 500,000 covers 120% and is 6.6% of direct cost, below the usual 10% but traceable.
  3. A monthly budget and S-curve, checked against the council's funding releases month by month.
  4. Numeric control rules: investigate if CPI or SPI falls below 0.95 or any control account varies by over AUD 25,000; escalate to the steering committee if residual EMV exceeds half the contingency (AUD 250,000).

质量（第 5 章）Quality (Chapter 5)

  1. 标准对准法规时间点。新版国家建筑规范 NCC 2025 从 2027 年 5 月 1 日起生效，正好落在工期中间，计划里写明以哪个版本为准、由认证师确认。
  2. 按关键交付物定质量要求、检验方法和停止点，区分质量保证（过程）和质量控制（结果）。
  3. 质量成本提前做进预算：预防类 4.5 万（无障碍和可持续设计审查、培训等）、检验类 12 万（审批检查、联合调试），而不是等出了问题再从不可预见费里扣。
  1. Standards tied to regulatory dates. NCC 2025 takes effect on 1 May 2027, mid-project, so the plan states which edition applies and who confirms it.
  2. Requirements, inspection methods and hold points for each key deliverable, separating assurance (process) from control (results).
  3. Cost of quality budgeted up front: AUD 45,000 prevention and AUD 120,000 appraisal, rather than paid later out of contingency.

资源（第 6 章）Resources (Chapter 6)

  1. 组织分解结构、角色职责、所需技能、人员获取与释放、冲突升级路径和 RACI 矩阵。
  2. 发现并修正一个漏洞。MS Project 里只记录了具体任务的工时。主体施工开始后，任务都分给了承包商，于是 2027 年 5 月的内部工作量掉到零，等于市政府没有任何人监督，和混合交付模式矛盾。我补了一份人员计划：项目经理和项目工程师全程全职，质量与调试经理从 2027 年 2 月进场，安全顾问在主体施工期按半职配置，保证每个月的计划人力都不低于任务需求。
  1. Organisation breakdown, roles, skills, acquisition and release, conflict escalation and a RACI matrix.
  2. Found and fixed a gap. MS Project recorded only task effort. Once main works began, tasks belonged to contractors, so in-house load fell to zero in May 2027: no council supervision at all, contradicting the hybrid model. I added a staffing plan: PM and Project Engineer full-time throughout, a Quality and Commissioning Manager from February 2027, and a WHS Adviser at 0.5 FTE during main works, so planned FTE never falls below task demand.

3. 关键判断3. Key judgements

  1. 6.6% 的不可预见费比 10% 更站得住。经验比例看着稳妥，但说不出为什么；用风险登记册算出来的数字，管理层可以追问到每一项风险。
  2. 内部人工要单独算清，再决定记在哪。内部人员约 5.8 人年、75 万澳元。如果施工期监督人员全记进间接成本，会超出 60 万的间接成本额度，所以把他们记到直接服务的施工账户里。
  3. 排程工具算出来的数字也要核对常识。「5 月内部人力为零」在软件里没有报错，但不符合项目实际。
  1. 6.6% contingency is more defensible than 10%. A rule of thumb sounds safe but cannot be explained; a figure from the risk register can be traced risk by risk.
  2. Cost in-house labour explicitly, then decide where it sits. About 5.8 FTE-years, AUD 750,000. Charging all construction-phase supervision to indirects would exceed the AUD 600,000 allowance, so it is charged to the construction accounts it serves.
  3. Sense-check what the scheduling tool says. Zero in-house load in May raised no error in the software, but made no sense for the project.

4. 不足与反思4. Limits and reflection

  1. 问题发现得太晚。人员为零的问题是在最后合稿时才发现的，因为小组按章节分工、各写各的。后来的 Scrum 作业里，问题两天内就能发现和修正。以后就算按章节分工，我也会在合稿前安排交叉审阅。
  2. 估算精度有限。当前是 3 级估算，精度范围 −20% 到 +30%，要等工料测量师审核和招标报价后才会收窄。
  3. 成绩还没公布，老师反馈出来后再补充。
  1. Found too late. The zero-staff gap only surfaced at final merge because we wrote chapters separately. In the later Scrum assignment, issues were found and fixed within two days. I now schedule a cross-review before any merge.
  2. Estimate accuracy. This is a Class 3 estimate (−20% to +30%), narrowing only after QS review and tender prices.
  3. The mark is pending; marker feedback will be added later.

5. 和实际工作的关系5. Why it matters in practice

在建设单位实习时，我接触过造价、结算和合同。这份计划让我从业主一侧完整做了一遍「预算怎么编、储备怎么定、谁来监督」。这些正是业主方项目管理岗位每天要回答的问题。

In my owner-side internship I worked on cost, final accounts and contracts. This plan let me work through, from the owner's side, how a budget is built, how reserves are set and who supervises: the everyday questions of an owner's project manager.
