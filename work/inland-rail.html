简介Summary

Inland Rail 是澳大利亚连接墨尔本和布里斯班的货运铁路。2026 年项目调整后，部分路段确定建设，北段只保留走廊、何时开工不确定，沿线土地所有者最关心的是「我的地会怎样」。小组为它设计一套新的沟通与参与策略，我负责把策略落到执行：分四步重建信任，以及每个关切由谁负责、多久必须回应。

Inland Rail is Australia's Melbourne–Brisbane freight line. After its 2026 reset, some sections are confirmed while the northern corridor is only preserved, with construction uncertain, and landholders mainly ask what it means for their land. Our group designed a new engagement strategy; I turned it into execution: four steps to rebuild trust, and who owns each concern and how fast they must respond.

1. 我负责的两页1. My two slides

2. 我的思路2. How I approached it

  1. 顺序比速度重要。四步依次是：0–2 周先把事实对齐，分清哪些已确定、哪些保留、哪些未知；2–4 周私下先告诉受影响的土地所有者，不让他们从新闻里得知；1–3 个月公开倾听，事先说明哪些意见能改变决定；之后每次会后发「你们说了什么，我们做了什么」。结论是：会慢一点，但前后不会自相矛盾，公开更新也不中断。
  2. 每个关切都要有人、有时限。三类负责人：沟通负责人签发项目口径，责任政府部门签发政府决定，联络官登记每个问题并写明回复日期。争议事项 48 小时内升级到高层发言人，强调是「升级」而不是让一线自己解决。只要出现新决定（资金、范围、走廊），所有对外口径同步更新。
  1. Sequence matters more than speed. Weeks 0–2 align facts (confirmed, preserved, unknown); weeks 2–4 tell affected landholders privately first so they do not learn from the news; months 1–3 listen publicly, stating what feedback can change; then a "you said, we did" record after every session. Slower, but never contradictory, and public updates never stop.
  2. Every concern gets an owner and a deadline. Three owners: a communications lead for project statements, the responsible authority for government decisions, and liaison officers who log each issue with a reply date. Contested issues escalate to an executive spokesperson within 48 hours: escalate, not resolve at the front line. Any new decision on funding, scope or corridor updates every briefing.

3. 和 Snowy 2.0 分析的联系3. Link to the Snowy 2.0 analysis

同一门课里我先做了 Snowy 2.0 的个人案例分析，结论是沟通在项目获批后断档、咨询没有形成闭环。这次小组展示里，我把那篇提出的「你们说了，我们做了」闭环和固定周期回应，直接落进了 Inland Rail 的执行步骤：前一个是找问题，这一个是给方案。

Earlier in the same unit I wrote the Snowy 2.0 case analysis, which found engagement stalled after approval and consultation never closed the loop. Here I built that paper's "you said, we did" loop and fixed-cycle responses directly into Inland Rail's execution steps: one diagnosed the problem, the other designs the fix.

4. 不足4. Limits

  1. 展示时间有限，四步方案没有配上资源和预算估算。
  2. 48 小时升级时限是设计值，没有找到同类项目的数据来验证是否合理。
  3. 成绩还没公布，老师反馈出来后再补充。
  1. Time limits meant the four steps were not costed or resourced.
  2. The 48-hour escalation window is a design choice not yet benchmarked against comparable projects.
  3. The mark is pending; feedback will be added later.
